# Code of conduct

adoken follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, critique code rather than people, and keep discussion on topic. Harassment,
insults, and personal or political attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
project space.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through the email address on their GitHub
profile ([@benzara-tahar](https://github.com/benzara-tahar)). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may
remove comments, close threads, or ban participants who break these rules.
